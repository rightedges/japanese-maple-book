# Appendix B: The Specialist's Essential Kit

To move from basic gardening to advanced stewardship, you need more than a standard pair of hardware-store shears. This kit represents the professional standard for Japanese Maple collectors.

## 1. Professional Cutting Tools
*   **Bypass Hand Pruners (Okatsune 103)**: The gold standard. Made of forged Izumo Yasugi steel. They make a clean "surgical" cut that allows the branch collar to heal instantly.
*   **Concave Cutter**: Essential for structural pruning. This specialized tool leaves a slightly hollowed-out wound that heals flat, preventing ugly "knobs" on the trunk.
*   **Folding Saw (Silky Gomboy)**: For branches larger than an inch. The impulse-hardened teeth provide a smooth cut that reduces the risk of fungal infection.

## 2. The Propagation Kit
*   **Grafting Knife (Right-handed bevel)**: Must be kept razor-sharp. A single, clean slice is the difference between a successful graft and a failure.
*   **Parafilm M**: A self-sealing, stretchy tape used to wrap grafts. It holds moisture in but allows the buds to "poke through" when they wake up.
*   **Bottom Heat Mat**: For cuttings and seeds. Maintaining a steady 75°F (24°C) at the root zone is the secret to high success rates.

## 3. High-Pressure Misting Gear
*   **0.3mm Misting Nozzles**: For high-level VPD management.
*   **Automatic Interval Timer**: Must be capable of "seconds" (e.g., 60 seconds on / 60 minutes off) rather than just hours or days.

## 4. The Specialized Apothecary
*   **Seaweed/Kelp Extract (Liquid)**: A root-hormone boost for stressed trees.
*   **Top-Jin M (Paste)**: A professional Japanese wound sealant. It contains a fungicide and creates a flexible, waterproof seal over major cuts.
*   **Copper-Based Fungicide**: Essential for treating *Pseudomonas* (Bacterial Blight).
*   **Mycorrhizae Inoculant**: Beneficial fungi to "charge" new soil mixes, especially for container trees.

## 5. Drainage Materials
*   **Horticultural Pumice**: Far superior to perlite. It doesn't float, it doesn't crush, and it provides permanent air-pockets in the soil.
*   **Pine Bark Fines**: The foundation of the "Long-Term" maple mix.
