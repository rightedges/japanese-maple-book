## Overview
'Sister Ghost' is a premier member of Flora Wonder’s "Ghost" series, prized for its extraordinary reticulated variegation. Among its more dramatic kin like 'Amber Ghost' or 'Purple Ghost', 'Sister Ghost' offers a more subtle, cool-toned, and ethereal presence. It is legendary for its "misty" appearance, where creamy chartreuse leaves are overlaid with an intricate network of deep emerald-green veins.

The tree has a graceful, upright, and somewhat bushy habit that matures into an elegant specimen reaching 8-12 feet. It is a tree of immense sophistication, appearing like a "green flame" in the shade of the garden.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: A breathtaking emergence. New leaves burst forth in a soft, bright chartreuse or creamy-yellow, with a stark, dark network of deep green veins. The reticulation is at its peak in early spring, creating a visual effect that is truly unique and "ghostly."
- **Summer**: The contrast between the veins and leaf field remains highly visible, providing continued interest and a "stained-glass" effect when backlit by the sun. The tree maintains a fresh, luminous look through the height of summer, provided it is protected from harsh sun.
- **Fall**: A reliable finale in shades of brilliant lemon-yellow, soft orange, and occasionally a warm gold. The fall display is exceptionally bright and provides a sophisticated contrast to its cool-toned spring appearance.
- **Architecture**: Develops a well-balanced, multi-stemmed structure that provides a powerful silhouette in the winter dormant season.

## Garden Use & Care
'Sister Ghost' should be used as a focal point in a woodland garden or a protected courtyard where its intricate leaf patterns can be appreciated up close. It is particularly effective when planted where it can catch the morning sun, which highlights the translucent quality of the reticulated leaves. It is also an exceptional candidate for large containers.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Protection**: Critical for success. Like most variegated maples, 'Sister Ghost' is sensitive to direct, harsh afternoon sun, which will cause the delicate chartreuse pigments to bleach or the leaf margins to "crisp." Plant in a spot with filtered light or bright, deep shade.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Requires rich, well-draining, slightly acidic soil. It is sensitive to "wet feet," so ensure the planting site is naturally well-drained or slightly mounded.
- **Minimal Pruning**: Naturally maintains a graceful, upright shape. Very little pruning is required, though a light structural thinning in late winter can help maintain its airy form and improve air circulation.

> [!TIP]
> Think of 'Sister Ghost' as a "living stained-glass window in the shade." Plant it where it can be backlit by the morning sun, and the intricate network of veins will glow with a cool intensity that is unmatched.
