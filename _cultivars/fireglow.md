## Overview
'Fireglow' is a standout red cultivar specifically selected for its "glow." While 'Bloodgood' and 'Emperor 1' tend towards deep burgundy, 'Fireglow' maintains a brighter, more saturated translucent red that appears to be lit from within when the sun catches it. It is also noted for being slightly more compact and refined than its larger cousins, making it an excellent choice for mid-sized gardens.

The tree has a strong, upright, and somewhat bushy habit. It is exceptionally sun-tolerant and is often cited as one of the best reds for areas with intense heat, as it handles the sun without leaf scorch or significant color fading.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: New leaves emerge in a brilliant, glowing ruby-red. The foliage is slightly smaller and more delicate than 'Bloodgood', giving the tree a refined, high-quality appearance early in the season.
- **Summer**: Remarkable for its color retention. 'Fireglow' stays a vivid, saturated red through the height of summer, rarely "bronzing" out. The leaves have a thin, translucent quality that allows light to pass through, creating the "glowing" effect for which it is named.
- **Fall**: A dramatic finish in shades of intense crimson and fire-engine red. The fall display is exceptionally bright and provides a long-lasting show of warm, saturated color.

## Garden Use & Care
'Fireglow' is an ideal specimen for a medium-sized garden, a focal point in a mixed border, or as a structural element near a patio. Its slightly smaller stature (10-12 feet) and intense color make it an excellent choice for framing a door or as a central element in a courtyard. It also performs exceptionally well in large containers.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Light Requirements**: This is a true sun-lover. To get the best "glow," plant it where it receives significant direct light. It handles full sun in most climates (Zones 5-8) with ease.
- **Watering**: Despite its sun tolerance, it prefers consistent moisture. The translucent leaves can be sensitive to extreme drought, so ensure it remains well-hydrated during dry spells.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Requires well-draining, slightly acidic soil. Like all Palmatums, it is sensitive to "wet feet," so ensure the planting site is naturally well-drained.

> [!TIP]
> Plant 'Fireglow' where it can be backlit by the morning or afternoon sun. The way the light passes through the "glowing" ruby leaves is one of the most beautiful sights in the maple world.
