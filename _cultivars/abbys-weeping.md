## Overview
'Abby's Weeping' is a remarkable and rare dwarf cultivar of *Acer japonicum* (the Full Moon maple). While most *japonicum* maples are robust, upright trees, 'Abby's Weeping' breaks the mold with a graceful, cascading growth habit. This makes it highly sought after for small spaces, water features, and containers.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: The large, deeply divided, lace-like leaves emerge green, often carrying a delicate bronze tint.
- **Summer**: The foliage matures to a solid, rich green. The texture of the broad, incised leaves provides a lush, woodland feel.
- **Fall**: True to its *japonicum* heritage, the autumn display is nothing short of spectacular. The foliage transitions into vibrant, multi-tonal shades of yellow, bright orange, fiery red, and sometimes deep purple.
- **Form**: A true dwarf with a cascading, umbrella-like form. It typically only reaches 2 to 3 feet in height and 3 to 4 feet in width over a 10-year period, unless staked higher.

## Garden Use & Care
Because of its small stature and weeping form, 'Abby's Weeping' is perfect for trailing over boulders, retaining walls, or the edges of a decorative container. It provides the extraordinary fall color of a standard *japonicum* in a fraction of the space.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Tolerance**: Performs best in dappled shade or morning sun. Protection from harsh afternoon sun is recommended to prevent leaf scorch, as its wide leaves can lose moisture quickly.
- **Soil & Moisture**: Prefers moist, rich, slightly acidic, and well-draining soil. Do not let it dry out completely during the hot summer months.

> [!TIP]
> If you want your 'Abby's Weeping' to gain more height before it cascades, you must actively train and stake the central leader during its first few years of growth. Left unstaked, it will remain a low, sprawling mound.
