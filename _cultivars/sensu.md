## Overview
'Sensu', which translates to "Folding Fan," is a spectacular and relatively rare cultivar prized for its extraordinary leaf shape and exceptional fall color. The leaves are deeply cut with many lobes, often 9 to 11, giving them a broad, fan-like appearance that bridges the gap between the Palmatum and Shirasawanum groups. It is a vigorous, upright tree reaching 10-15 feet, making it a true centerpiece for any landscape.

The tree develops a strong, architectural presence with upward-sweeping branches. It is exceptionally sun-tolerant and holds its foliage with a pristine, deep-green quality through the height of summer before igniting in its famous autumn display.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: New leaves emerge in a bright, clean chartreuse or light green, often with subtle pink or orange highlights on the margins. The "folding fan" texture is immediately apparent as the multi-lobed leaves unfold early in the season.
- **Summer**: Superior leaf health. 'Sensu' maintains its bright emerald-green color flawlessly through the heat of summer. The tree forms a dense, airy canopy that provides excellent translucent light.
- **Fall**: A breathtaking grand finale. The foliage transitions into a brilliant display of electric orange, burnt gold, and saturated scarlet-red. The fall color is exceptionally vivid and provides a long-lasting show of fire-like tones.
- **Architecture**: Develops a well-balanced, multi-stemmed structure that provides a powerful silhouette in the winter dormant season.

## Garden Use & Care
'Sensu' is a premier specimen for a mid-sized garden, a lawn centerpiece, or as a structural element near a patio where its unique "fan" texture can be appreciated up close. Its fast growth rate (up to 12-18 inches/year) and vibrant color make it an excellent choice for those who want a significant presence quickly.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Tolerance**: This is one of the most sun-tolerant green cultivars in existence. In most climates (Zones 5-8), it can handle full sun without significant leaf burn, provided it has consistent moisture.
- **Space Management**: Give this tree plenty of vertical and horizontal room. Its large leaves and vigor mean it will eventually command a significant amount of space.
- **Minimal Pruning**: Its natural form is strong and well-balanced. Very little structural pruning is required, though a light structural thinning in late winter can help maintain air circulation.

> [!TIP]
> Think of 'Sensu' as a "more robust Shirasawanum look." It provides the same fan-like elegance of the Golden Full Moon maples but with much greater sun tolerance and vigor.
