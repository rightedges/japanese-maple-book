## Overview
'Tsuri-no-mai' (sometimes spelled 'Tsuru-no-mai') is a stunning variegated cultivar known for its painterly, almost watercolored foliage. Often translated to mean "dancing crane," its delicate variegation and elegant upright posture make it a prized addition to smaller spaces or container gardens.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: Leaves emerge with a brilliant base of white and green, vividly accented by delicate pink edges and bright pink stems.
- **Summer**: As temperatures rise, the pink tones typically fade, leaving a crisp and clean variegated pattern of cream-white and green that holds well throughout the summer.
- **Fall**: In autumn, the foliage transitions to vibrant, fiery shades of orange and red.
- **Form**: A slow-growing, upright deciduous tree that typically reaches a compact 5 to 8 feet, though very mature specimens may eventually grow taller.

## Garden Use & Care
Its naturally compact size makes 'Tsuri-no-mai' an excellent candidate for container culture or as a focal point in a small courtyard garden.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Tolerance**: Thrives best in morning sun with complete protection from the intense afternoon sun. This protects the delicate white and pink variegation from leaf scorch.
- **Soil & Moisture**: Prefers rich, well-draining, slightly acidic soil. Regular watering is essential for young trees to maintain the health of the variegated leaves.

> [!TIP]
> Variegated maples like 'Tsuri-no-mai' contain less chlorophyll than solid-colored varieties, which is why they are slower-growing and slightly more sensitive to extreme heat and drought.
