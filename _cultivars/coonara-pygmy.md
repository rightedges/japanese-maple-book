## Overview
'Coonara Pygmy' is a delightful dwarf cultivar originally discovered as a witch's broom in Australia. It is prized for its dense, twiggy, globe-like habit and small, delicate leaves. Despite its dwarf classification, it is relatively vigorous for its size, quickly forming a lush, architectural "mound" in the garden.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: One of the earliest to wake. New growth emerge in soft shades of pale pink, gold, and light orange, giving the dense canopy a glowing, multi-colored appearance.
- **Summer**: The tiny five-lobed leaves mature to a crisp, bright green. The dense, overlapping foliage creates a solid texture that holds its color well through the season.
- **Fall**: A standout performer. The autumn display is a vibrant mix of yellow, orange, and brilliant scarlet-red, often holding its color for several weeks.

## Garden Use & Care
'Coonara Pygmy' is exceptionally versatile. Its compact size (3-6 feet) makes it perfect for rock gardens, small residential entranceways, and low-maintenance foundation plantings. It is also a premier choice for long-term container gardening and bonsai.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Siting**: Prefers morning sun and afternoon shade. While it can tolerate full sun in cooler zones, dappled shade helps preserve the soft pinks of its spring growth.
- **Watering**: Requires consistent, even moisture. Its dense foliage can sometimes shed rain away from the root ball, so ensure targeted watering during dry spells.
- **Minimal Pruning**: Due to its slow growth and naturally rounded habit, very little pruning is required. Remove any interior deadwood in late winter to maintain air circulation.

> [!TIP]
> Use 'Coonara Pygmy' in a large pot as a living sculpture. Its naturally rounded shape requires almost no help to remain a perfect sphere of seasonal color.
