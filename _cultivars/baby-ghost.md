## Overview
'Baby Ghost' is a star member of the famous "Ghost Series" of reticulated maples developed by the legendary Talon Buchholz. As the name suggest, it is often cited as the smallest or shortest member of the series, making it highly valuable for small gardens and high-end container culture. It offers the same high-contrast, veined leaf pattern as its larger siblings ('Amber Ghost', 'Sister Ghost', etc.) but in a more manageable and structurally dense package.

The tree develops a rounded, spreading canopy that provides a shimmering, ethereal presence in the garden, especially when planted in dappled shade.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: A spectacular emergence. New leaves burst forth in unique shades of pink, apricot, or a deep "amber-red." Each leaf is prominently accented by dark green or burgundy veins, providing an "electric" appearance in the early spring light.
- **Summer**: The spring colors transition into a sophisticated reddish-purple or a deep bronzed-green. The reticulated veins remain visible, giving the leaf a textured, three-dimensional look.
- **Fall**: A breathtaking finale of bright orange and fiery scarlet. The fall display is multi-toned and provides a powerful contrast to its spring palette.
- **Form**: Naturally develops into a small, upright, and somewhat bushy tree reaching 6-10 feet. It is a slow grower that holds its shape remarkably well.

## Garden Use & Care
'Baby Ghost' is a premier specimen for a protected courtyard, a woodland garden border, or as a standalone focal point near a sitting area. Its compact size also makes it one of the best variegated maples for long-term container gardening on a patio or balcony. It pairs beautifully with solid-green companions or low-growing blue hostas.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Protection**: Performs best in morning sun and filtered afternoon shade. While it can handle more sun than some other Ghosts, protecting it from the harshest afternoon heat is key to maintaining the best leaf color and preventing tip burn.
- **Moisture**: Requires consistent moisture. Like all members of the Ghost series, it is sensitive to drought. Mulching at the base will help keep the roots cool and moist.
- **Graft Monitoring**: Monitor for and remove any new growth appearing on the rootstock below the graft union, as these vigorous "wild" shoots can quickly overtake the delicate variegated foliage.

> [!TIP]
> think of 'Baby Ghost' as "personality in a small package." If you love the look of the Ghost Series but only have a small space, this is your tree. It glows in the morning light like no other.
