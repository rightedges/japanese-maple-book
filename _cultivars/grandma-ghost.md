## Overview
'Grandma Ghost' is a highly sought-after cultivar belonging to the famous "Ghost" series developed by Talon Buchholz. Known for its ethereal glow and delicate coloring, it brightens up shady woodland gardens and serves as an exquisite specimen plant.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: Unfurls with delicate, cream-colored foliage accented by deeply contrasting, dark green reticulated (net-like) veins. The effect is profoundly ghost-like and illuminating.
- **Summer**: The cream tones slowly darken to a soft light green, though the deeper green veining remains distinct.
- **Fall**: The tree transforms completely, putting on a spectacular show of vibrant orange and vivid crimson-red tones.
- **Form**: Moderate grower that typically forms a balanced, upright tree reaching 8–10 feet in height.

## Garden Use & Care
'Grandma Ghost' shines as an understory tree or a woodland edge centerpiece. Because of its light-colored foliage, it can visibly "lighten" a dark corner of the garden.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Tolerance**: Needs protection from intense, direct afternoon sun. Extended direct sun will scorch the cream-colored leaves, so filtered or dappled shade is strongly recommended.
- **Placement**: Pair with dark companion plants (like dark hostas or evergreen azaleas) to emphasize its ethereal pale spring color.

> [!TIP]
> The intricate, net-like venation of the "Ghost" series maples is an evolutionary adaptation that gives them a stunning textured appearance, but it also signals a need for a sheltered, moist environment.
