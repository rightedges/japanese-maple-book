## Overview
'Mila' is a relatively recent and highly regarded introduction from Holland, discovered by the esteemed maple grower Dick van der Maat. It is prized for its extreme vigor and its ability to produce vibrant, ever-changing foliage colors throughout the entire growing season. Unlike many red cultivars that settle into a single shade, 'Mila' is dynamic, frequently producing "flushes" of new, bright red growth over a dark, established base.

The tree develops a strong, upright vase shape with a well-structured and dense canopy. It is more robust than many other red cultivars, making it a "reliable" tree for those who want a significant presence in the garden quickly.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: A spectacular emergence. New leaves burst forth in vibrant, pastel pink-to-red tones, often featuring a distinct orange or chartreuse "eye" or center. The overall effect is soft, luminous, and incredibly dense.
- **Summer**: The spring pinks mature into a sophisticated olive green or dark burgundy. However, the tree is a "heavy flusher," meaning it will continue to produce clusters of bright maroon or ruby-red new growth at the tips of the branches throughout the summer.
- **Fall**: A breathtaking finale in shades of brilliant orange and fire-engine red. The autumn display is exceptionally bright and long-lasting, providing a high-impact show.
- **Form**: Develops a well-balanced, multi-stemmed structure that provides a powerful silhouette in the winter landscape.

## Garden Use & Care
'Mila' is a premier specimen for a mid-sized garden, a lawn centerpiece, or as a structural element near a patio. Its fast growth rate (up to 18 inches/year in youth) and vibrant color make it an excellent choice for those who want to fill a structural gap quickly. It pairs beautifully with yellow-leaved companions like 'Jordan' or 'Summer Gold'.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Tolerance**: Surprisingly sun-tolerant for such a vibrant tree. In most climates (Zones 5-8), it can handle significant morning and midday sun, though it appreciates a break from the harshest afternoon heat to preserve the best color.
- **Space Management**: Give this tree room. Its vigor and dense habit mean it will eventually command a significant amount of vertical and horizontal space.
- **Minimal Pruning**: Its natural form is strong and well-balanced. Minimal structural pruning is required, though a light structural thinning in late winter can help maintain air circulation.

> [!TIP]
> think of 'Mila' as a "color engine." Most maples have two big shows (Spring and Fall), but 'Mila' produces constant flushes of new Ruby-colored growth all summer long.
