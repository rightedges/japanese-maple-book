## Overview
'Purple Ghost' is arguably the most spectacular and popular member of Flora Wonder’s "Ghost" series. It is legendary for its dramatic reticulated variegation, where deep purple or burgundy leaves are overlaid with an intricate network of prominent black-red veins. It is a tree of immense sophistication and beauty, appearing like a royal tapestry in the garden.

The tree has a graceful, upright, and somewhat bushy habit. It is more vigorous and sun-tolerant than the white-leaved members of the Ghost series, making it a more accessible choice for a variety of landscape settings.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: A horticultural masterpiece. New leaves emerge in a brilliant magenta-red with a stark, dark network of black veins. The reticulation is at its peak in early spring, creating a visual effect that is truly unique and high-impact.
- **Summer**: The magenta tones deepen into a sophisticated "garnet" or deep burgundy. The dark veins remain highly visible, providing continued interest and a "stained-glass" effect when backlit by the sun.
- **Fall**: A dramatic finale in shades of brilliant orange and fire-engine red. The fall display is exceptionally vivid and provides a long-lasting show that stands out even among the best red cultivars.

## Garden Use & Care
'Purple Ghost' should be used as a focal point where its intricate leaf patterns can be appreciated up close. It is particularly effective when planted where it can catch the morning or afternoon sun, which highlights the translucent quality of the reticulated leaves. It is also an exceptional candidate for large containers.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Light Requirements**: Finding the "Goldilocks" zone of light is key. To get the best purple-black reticulation, provide several hours of morning sun. However, avoid intense late-afternoon sun in hot climates to prevent leaf scorch.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Requires rich, well-draining, slightly acidic soil. Like all variegated maples, it is sensitive to "wet feet," so ensure the planting site is naturally well-drained.
- **Minimal Pruning**: Very little pruning is required. A light thinning of the interior in late winter can help reveal the elegant branching pattern and improve air circulation.

> [!TIP]
> imagine 'Purple Ghost' as a "living stained-glass window." Plant it where it can be backlit by the sun, and the intricate network of veins will glow with a royal intensity that is unmatched.
