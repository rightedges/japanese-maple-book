## Overview
'Ukigumo', which translates to "Floating Cloud," is one of the most spectacular and highly sought-after variegated cultivars in the world. It is legendary for its extraordinarily white foliage, where many leaves are almost entirely devoid of chlorophyll, appearing like misty white clouds in the garden. It is a tree of immense delicacy and beauty, requiring specific conditions to maintain its high-contrast variegation.

The tree has a graceful, upright, and somewhat bushy habit. It is a slow to moderate grower, eventually forming a dense, architectural specimen that is highly prized by collectors of variegated maples.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: A breathtaking emergence. New leaves burst forth in pure white or creamy-yellow, often with subtle pink highlights on the tips. The variegation is at its most intense in early spring, creating a visual effect that is truly unique and "ghostly."
- **Summer**: The white tones slowly mature into a sophisticated mix of cream and soft green. In high-quality shade, the tree maintains a fresh, luminous look through the summer. Some leaves may stay almost pure white while others develop a fine "salt-and-pepper" dusting of green.
- **Fall**: A reliable finale in shades of bright yellow, burnt orange, and often a soft, warm red. The fall display is multi-toned and provide a sophisticated contrast to its ethereal spring appearance.

## Garden Use & Care
'Ukigumo' should be used as a focal point in a woodland garden or a protected courtyard where its intricate leaf patterns can be appreciated up close. It is particularly effective when planted against a dark background, such as a yew hedge or a dark stone wall, where its "floating white" leaves can truly shine.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Protection**: Critical for success. This cultivar is extremely sensitive to direct sun, which will cause the white pigments to bleach or the leaf margins to "crisp." Plant in a spot with filtered light or bright, deep shade.
- **Stability**: It is normal for 'Ukigumo' to occasionally produce all-green branches or leaves. If a branch stays all green, prune it out to maintain the tree's health and variegated genetics.
- **Moisture**: Requires consistent, even moisture. The delicate, white leaves have very little protective "wax," making the tree more sensitive to drought stress than most standard Palmatums.

> [!TIP]
> think of 'Ukigumo' as a "living cloud." Plant it where it can catch the gentlest morning light, and it will Reward you with an ethereal beauty that is unmatched in the maple world.
