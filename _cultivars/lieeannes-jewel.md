## Overview
'Lieeanne's jewel' is a unique and somewhat rare selection that brings an elegant, classic upright form to the garden. While not as widely distributed in commercial nurseries as standard cultivars, it is prized by collectors who appreciate its robust growth and subtle beauty. 

With its classic deeply lobed green leaves in summer and warm yellow-orange hues in autumn, it embodies the timeless appeal of the *Acer palmatum* species.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: Emerges with fresh, bright green foliage, sometimes carrying a slight reddish or bronze tinge at the margins before settling into a deep summer green.
- **Summer**: The leaves mature to a vibrant, solid green. It has a relatively sturdy leaf structure that holds up well during the warmer months, though it appreciates some afternoon protection in very hot climates.
- **Fall**: Transitions to a warm, glowing display of yellow and orange, lighting up darker corners of the landscape.
- **Form**: Forms a well-structured, upright tree that slowly spreads into a graceful, rounded canopy as it matures.

## Garden Use & Care
This cultivar is highly versatile and works perfectly as a small shade tree or a standalone specimen in a mixed border. Its classic green summer color makes it an excellent backdrop for brightly colored perennials or contrasting maples like 'Bloodgood'.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun/Color Relationship**: While it can handle a fair amount of sun, providing dappled afternoon shade will keep the foliage looking its best through late summer and prevent any tip scorch.
- **Soil & Drainage**: As with most Japanese maples, well-drained, slightly acidic soil is essential. Ensure the planting site doesn't hold standing water during winter.

> [!TIP]
> Rare and local selections like 'Lieeanne's jewel' can sometimes behave slightly differently depending on the microclimate. Observe its first year carefully to determine if it needs a bit more water or shade during the peak of summer!
