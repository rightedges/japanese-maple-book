## Overview
'Gloria' is a red *Acer shirasawanum* full-moon hybrid, a rarity among full moons which are more often green or gold. New growth emerges bright red and matures to deep maroon, carried on the broad, fan-like full-moon leaf. The habit is fairly dense and bushy, reaching 10-12 ft in about 15 years in Zones 5-9.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: Broad full-moon leaves emerge bright red, holding well into early summer.
- **Summer**: Foliage deepens to maroon. Best color with morning sun and protection from harsh afternoon heat.
- **Fall**: Turns a bright, luminous crimson — one of the strongest red fall displays in the Shirasawanum group.

## Garden Use & Care
Use as a compact upright specimen where a red full moon is wanted without the size of a large palmatum. Effective as a focal point against gold or green companions, and suitable for a large container.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Light Requirements**: Morning sun with afternoon shade in hot climates; more sun tolerated in cooler zones.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Well-draining, slightly acidic soil. Avoid wet feet, as with all Shirasawanums.
- **Pruning**: Minimal. Remove crossing or dead wood in late winter to preserve the dense, layered form.

> [!TIP]
> Pair 'Gloria' with a golden full moon such as 'Aureum' or 'Autumn Moon' — the red-to-gold contrast highlights the unusually large leaves both cultivars share.
