## Overview
'Celebration' is a remarkable cultivar prized for its striking reticulated variegation and incredibly vibrant seasonal color changes. Part of the broader "ghost" style of Japanese maples, it features prominent vein patterns that contrast beautifully against the softer base colors of its leaves. It matures into a broad, vase-shaped canopy, creating a focal point in any landscape.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: Leaves emerge in a burst of bright pink to vivid pink-red, with darker red veins running like a net across each lobe.
- **Summer**: The intense pinks fade to a greenish or bronze-purple color, yet the distinctive reticulated dark veins remain clearly visible.
- **Fall**: Transitions to brilliant, fiery shades of scarlet and fuchsia-red.
- **Form**: Develops an upright, vase-shaped structure reaching roughly 8–10 feet in height over ten years.

## Garden Use & Care
Due to its bright colors and dramatic shifts, 'Celebration' is popular among collectors as a focal point specimen. It adds incredible "movement" and texture to the garden.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Tolerance**: While described as somewhat sun-tolerant for a variegated cultivar, it still performs best with protection from harsh afternoon sun to prevent the delicate pink edges from scorching. Dappled shade is ideal.
- **Soil & Moisture**: Prefers moist, well-draining, and slightly acidic soil. 

> [!TIP]
> To maintain the brightest colors in the veins, ensure 'Celebration' gets a few hours of gentle morning sun, followed by dappled shade for the rest of the day.
