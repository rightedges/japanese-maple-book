## Overview
'Higasayama', which translates to "Mount East," is one of the oldest and most spectacular variegated cultivars in existence. It is famous for its unique leaf emergence, where the tightly furled buds look like tiny, multi-colored paintbrushes. As they open, the leaves unfold like miniature fans, revealing a complex tapestry of pink, cream, and green. It is a robust and vigorous upright tree, reaching 15-20 feet at maturity.

The tree has a strong, architectural presence with upward-sweeping branches. It is significantly more sun-tolerant than many other variegated maples, making it a reliable choice for larger landscapes where higher-impact variegation is desired.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: The most iconic phase. Tightly furled buds emerge in a brilliant magenta-pink. As they unfold, the leaves reveal a cream-white border with a green center, still heavily flushed with pink. The layered, "stacked" appearance of the emerging leaves is truly unique.
- **Summer**: The pink recedes, maturing into a crisp, clean variegated pattern of creamy white margins on a deep emerald-green center. The tree maintains a dense, healthy canopy that stays vibrant through the heat of summer.
- **Fall**: A dependable display of bright, clear yellow and soft orange. The variegated margins often turn a soft, "parchment" yellow while the centers become a saturated gold, creating a multi-toned autumn show.
- **Bark**: Older wood develops a refined, slightly fissured texture that adds structural interest in the winter months.

## Garden Use & Care
'Higasayama' is a powerful specimen tree that deserves a central position in the landscape. Because of its height (15-20 feet), it works perfectly as a lawn specimen or as a high-impact element in a large mixed border. Its upright, vase-like habit also makes it an excellent choice for a tall privacy screen or a "gatekeeper" tree at an entrance.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Exposure**: Handles sun better than almost any other variegated maple. To get the best cream-white variegation and the most intense spring pinks, provide at least several hours of direct morning or midday sun.
- **Reversion Check**: Like all variegated cultivars, it can occasionally produce solid green branches. Prune these out immediately to maintain the tree's health and variegated genetics.
- **Structural Pruning**: Because it is vigorous, perform light structural thinning in late winter to maintain its elegant, upward-sweeping form and ensure good air circulation through the canopy.

> [!TIP]
> Watch the buds carefully in early spring. The "paintbrush" stage of 'Higasayama' is one of the most photographed moments in the maple world—it's a literal celebration of the new season.
