## Overview
'Sekka Yatsubusa' is one of the most unique and bizarre dwarf cultivars in existence. It is legendary for its "fasciated" or "cockscomb" branches, where the stems flatten out and become unusually wide and congested, resembling the comb of a rooster. This creates a dense, architectural texture that is absolutely unique in the maple world. It is an extremely slow grower, eventually forming a rugged, sculptural specimen that looks ancient even when young.

The tree develops a complex, multi-stemmed habit with short, stout, and flattened branches. Its structural beauty is so intense that it remains a focal point even in the winter dormant season.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: New leaves emerge in a bright chartreuse or lime-green. The "congested" effect is immediately apparent as the leaves unfold in tight, overlapping clusters along the flattened branches. The emerging foliage has a fresh, luminous quality.
- **Summer**: The foliage matures into a solid, healthy mid-green. Because the leaves are so densely packed on the fasciated stems, the tree creates a solid "mass" of texture that holds its color well through the summer heat.
- **Fall**: A reliable finale. The foliage transitions into a beautiful tapestry of golden yellow and soft red. The fall display is multi-toned and echoing its refined texture.
- **Winter Silhoutte**: The flattened, "cockscomb" branches are the true star of the winter landscape, providing a stunning and unique silhouette that is unmatched by more traditional maples.

## Garden Use & Care
'Sekka Yatsubusa' is the premier choice for rock gardens, small residential entranceways, and as a "living sculpture" in a stone garden. Its extremely slow growth and small size (2-4 feet) make it a perfect candidate for long-term container gardening and high-quality bonsai. It should be planted where its unique "cockscomb" texture can be appreciated up close.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Light Shielding**: Morning sun and afternoon shade is the ideal balance. While it handles light well, the best health is preserved by providing protection from the most intense afternoon heat.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Requires perfectly draining, slightly acidic soil. Like all dwarf cultivars, it is sensitive to "wet feet," so ensure the planting site is naturally well-drained.
- **Minimal Pruning**: Very little pruning is required. Its naturally rugged, fasciated habit is self-maintaining. Focus on removing any interior deadwood in late winter to maintain air circulation through the dense foliage.

> [!TIP]
> Think of 'Sekka Yatsubusa' as a "natural curiosity." Plant it near a stone lantern or a decorative basin to create an instant, high-quality Japanese garden vignette.
