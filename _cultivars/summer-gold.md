## Overview
'Summer Gold' is a revolutionary cultivar that has finally solved the "yellow leaf" problem. Historically, yellow-leaved maples like 'Aureum' were extremely fragile and required deep shade. 'Summer Gold' is the first yellow-leaved Palmatum that can handle full sun with ease, maintaining a shocking, luminous lemon-yellow color even in the height of summer. It is prized for its extraordinary durability and its ability to act as a "500-watt light bulb" in the garden.

The tree has a graceful, upright, and somewhat bushy habit. It is more vigorous and resilient than almost any other yellow cultivar, making it an accessible choice for high-impact illumination in any landscape.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: New leaves emerge in a soft chartreuse or light lemon-yellow. The foliage is fresh and luminous, immediately brightening its corner of the garden.
- **Summer**: Outstanding color stability. Unlike other yellows that turn green or burn in the sun, 'Summer Gold' intensifies its color, becoming a solid, saturated lemon-yellow. It maintains this "neon" presence throughout the most punishing summer heat.
- **Fall**: A reliable and beautiful display of brilliant yellow, burnt orange, and soft red. The fall color is exceptionally vivid and echoes the warm tones of its summer display.
- **Luminosity**: The tree appears to capture and hold the light, glowing even on overcast days.

## Garden Use & Care
'Summer Gold' should be used as a focal point where its "illumination" is needed. It works beautifully as a specimen plant, providing a warm focal point in full-sun settings. Its upright habit also makes it an excellent choice for a mid-sized mixed border or as a background for darker-leaved cultivars like 'Bloodgood'. It is also an exceptional candidate for large containers.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Tolerance**: This tree LOVES the sun. To get the best lemon-yellow color, plant it in a full sun position. It handles heat with significantly more grace than almost any other yellow-leaf cultivar.
- **Watering**: Despite its sun tolerance, it prefers consistent moisture. While it doesn't burn easily, drought stress can lead to smaller leaves or muted color.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Requires well-draining, slightly acidic soil. Like all Palmatums, it is sensitive to "wet feet," so ensure the planting site is naturally well-drained.

> [!TIP]
> Planting a 'Summer Gold' is the horticultural equivalent of turning on a 500-watt light bulb that never burns out in your sunny garden. Use it to "light up" a dark background or a grey stone wall.
