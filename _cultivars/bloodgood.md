## Overview
'Bloodgood' is arguably the most famous and widely planted Japanese maple in the world. Since its introduction, it has become the standard by which all other purple-leaved maples are judged. It is prized for its exceptional color retention—holding its deep burgundy-red hue through the heat of summer better than almost any other cultivar—and its robust, dependable growth habit.

The tree matures into a classic, upright vase shape with a broad, rounded canopy. It is exceptionally hardy and adaptable, making it the "gold standard" for both novice gardeners and professional landscapers alike.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: New leaves emerge in a deep, saturated purple-red. The foliage is large and healthy, quickly forming a dense, colorful canopy that defines the tree's presence for the rest of the season.
- **Summer**: One of its best traits is its color stability. While many red maples fade to bronze or green in high heat, 'Bloodgood' maintains its rich burgundy color remarkably well, especially when provided with full sun.
- **Fall**: A dramatic transformation into a brilliant, translucent scarlet-red. The fall color is exceptionally vivid and can remain on the tree for several weeks, providing a long-lasting and high-impact autumn show.
- **Form**: Develops a well-structured, multi-stemmed habit that provides excellent winter architecture after the leaves have fallen.

## Garden Use & Care
'Bloodgood' is a versatile "workhorse" specimen. It is powerful enough to stand alone as a lawn specimen, but it also works beautifully as a structural element in a large mixed border or as a background for lighter-colored cultivars. Its robust nature and relatively fast growth (12-18 inches/year) make it an ideal choice for those who want a significant presence in their landscape quickly.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Tolerance**: This is one of the best reds for full sun. In most temperate climates (Zones 5-7), it handles direct sun with ease. In very hot climates (Zones 8-9), some dappled afternoon shade is beneficial but not strictly necessary if consistent moisture is provided.
- **Soil & Moisture**: Prefers rich, well-draining, slightly acidic soil. While it is more resilient than many cultivars, it remains sensitive to "wet feet," so ensure the planting site is naturally well-drained or mounded.
- **Verticality**: Because it can reach 15-20 feet at maturity, ensure you give it enough vertical and horizontal space to grow into its natural vase shape without needing heavy structural pruning.

> [!TIP]
> If you are only planting one Japanese maple, 'Bloodgood' is the safest and most rewarding choice. It is the definition of "reliable beauty" in the maple world.
