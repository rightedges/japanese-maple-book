## Overview
'Kuro hime', which translates to "Black-Red Princess," is a relatively rare and highly prized dwarf cultivar. It is noted for its spectacular multi-toned appearance, featuring tiny leaves that emerge in a complex darker-pink or "black-red" hue before transitioning through a series of sophisticated colors. It has a dense, mounding habit that eventually forms a wide, architectural sphere reaching just 3-5 feet.

The tree has an exceptionally refined texture, with small, star-shaped leaves that are held in dense clusters along the branches. It is a slow grower, making it a "treasure" for collectors of dwarf maples and bonsai enthusiasts.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: A horticultural gem. New leaves emerge as a cloud of deep magenta-pink or "black-red." The emerging foliage is extremely small and intensely colored, creating a high-impact "blush" across the dense canopy.
- **Summer**: The foliage matures into a sophisticated green-and-pink tapestry. The older leaves become a healthy mid-green, while the continued new growth at the tips stays bright pink, providing a multi-layered, bi-color look throughout the summer.
- **Fall**: A dramatic grand finale in shades of saturated scarlet-red and orange. The fall color is exceptionally bright for a dwarf and provides a long-lasting show that stands out even among larger cultivars.
- **Form**: Develops a dense, twiggy, and well-structured mound that provides excellent winter architecture after the leaves have fallen.

## Garden Use & Care
Due to its small stature (3-5 feet) and high-quality detail, 'Kuro hime' is a premier choice for rock gardens, small residential courtyards, or as a focal point in a stone garden. It is also an exceptional candidate for long-term container gardening and bonsai, where its naturally compact habit and multi-toned leaves are highly prized.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Light Shielding**: Morning sun and afternoon shade is the ideal "Goldilocks" zone. While it handles light well, the best "black-red" and pink colors are preserved by providing protection from the most intense afternoon heat.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Requires perfectly draining, slightly acidic soil. Like most dwarf cultivars, it is sensitive to "wet feet," so ensure the planting site is naturally well-drained or slightly mounded.
- **Sanitation**: Because of its dense branching, 'Kuro hime' benefits from a light thinning of the interior in late winter to improve air circulation and prevent fungal issues in high-humidity climates.

> [!TIP]
> Think of 'Kuro hime' as a "living jewel." Plant it where you can appreciate its tiny, star-shaped leaves and shifting colors up close—perhaps near a seating area or a decorative water basin.
