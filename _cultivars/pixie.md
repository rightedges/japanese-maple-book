## Overview
'Pixie' is one of the most reliable and beloved dwarf red cultivars in the world. It is often called the "Miniature Bloodgood" because it offers the same deep burgundy foliage and robust nature of its famous parent but in a compact, upright package reaching only 4-6 feet. It is prized for its exceptional color retention and its ability to fit into the smallest garden spaces without losing its high-impact presence.

The tree has a naturally well-branched, bushy habit that develops into a dense, architectural specimen. It is faster growing than many other dwarfs, making it a "rewarding" choice for gardeners who want a significant presence quickly.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: New leaves emerge in a brilliant, shocking arrangement of burnt maroon and bright red. The foliage is relatively small and appears in dense clusters, creating a cloud of intense red early in the season.
- **Summer**: Outstanding color stability. 'Pixie' holds its dark burgundy-red color remarkably well through the heat of summer, rarely "bronzing" or turning green even in full sun settings.
- **Fall**: A dramatic finale. The foliage transitions into a brilliant, translucent fire-engine red. The fall display is exceptionally bright and provides a long-lasting show before the leaves drop.
- **Form**: Develops a dense, twiggy structure that provides excellent winter interest after the leaves have fallen.

## Garden Use & Care
Due to its small size (4-6 feet) and intense color, 'Pixie' is a premier choice for rock gardens, small residential courtyards, or as a focal point in a mixed border. It is also an exceptional candidate for bonsai and long-term container gardening, where its naturally compact habit and robust nature are highly prized.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Tolerance**: This is one of the best reds for full sun in the dwarf group. In most climates (Zones 5-8), it can handle direct sun with ease, provided it has consistent moisture.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Requires well-draining, slightly acidic soil. Like all Palmatums, it is sensitive to "wet feet," so ensure the planting site is naturally well-drained.
- **Minimal Pruning**: Very little pruning is required. Its naturally bushy habit is self-maintaining, but a light thinning of the interior in late winter can help maintain air circulation.

> [!TIP]
> If you love 'Bloodgood' but only have a small patio or a narrow garden bed, 'Pixie' is the perfect solution. It delivers that legendary red-maple look in a "pixie-sized" portion.
