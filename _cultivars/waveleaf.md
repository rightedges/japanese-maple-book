## Overview
'Waveleaf' is a highly distinct cultivar introduced by Talon Buchholz in Oregon. It combines the fascinating reticulated (veined) patterns found in the "Ghost" maples with a uniquely textured leaf structure, creating a dynamic, almost sculptural presence in the landscape.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: Foliage emerges with a bright pink-red coloration that quickly darkens, heavily highlighted by dark, almost black veining.
- **Summer**: Settles into a dark red or burgundy color, retaining its striking reticulation. The signature trait of this tree is its leaf structure: the lobes are slightly curled and have wavy edges, giving the canopy a textured, ruffled appearance.
- **Fall**: Finishes the season with a dramatic display of crimson red.
- **Form**: Upright growth habit, reaching roughly 8 to 10 feet in height within ten years.

## Garden Use & Care
'Waveleaf' provides an extraordinary textural contrast when planted near smooth, broad-leafed evergreens or fine-textured green maples. Its unique leaf shape makes it a conversation piece in any maple collection.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Tolerance**: Like many reticulated and heavily textured maples, 'Waveleaf' prefers filtered light or morning sun with protection against harsh afternoon sun to prevent leaf scorch.
- **Soil & Moisture**: Thrives in moist, humus-rich, and well-draining soil. Consistent moisture during the hot summer months will help maintain the health of its uniquely shaped leaves.

> [!TIP]
> Do not confuse this maple with the "Wave-Leaf Jade" succulent. This 'Waveleaf' is a true *Acer palmatum* that demands the same meticulous watering and soil drainage practices as other premium maples.
