## Overview
'Hippi-fi-mo' (often spelled 'Hippy Fin Mo') is a truly unique dwarf Japanese maple that is highly sought after for its incredible leaf texture. It belongs to the group of maples featuring rolled or curled leaves, somewhat similar to 'Okushimo' or 'Kurui jishi', but on a much more compact frame.

The leaves are small, extremely glossy, and curl upward at the edges, creating a three-dimensional, crinkled appearance that adds a completely different textural element to a maple collection. 

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: The curled leaves emerge a bright, shiny green, immediately showing off their distinctive rolled margins.
- **Summer**: The foliage matures to a deep kelly green. The glossy surface of the leaves catches the light beautifully, making the tree stand out even in shaded areas. 
- **Fall**: Puts on a bright autumn display of yellow and orange tones, contrasting wonderfully with its unique leaf shape.
- **Form**: A slow-growing dwarf that typically maintains an upright, somewhat bushy habit. It is an excellent choice for small spaces where its texture can be appreciated up close.

## Garden Use & Care
Because of its compact size and intricate details, 'Hippi-fi-mo' is perfectly suited for container gardening, courtyard gardens, or as a focal point near a patio or walkway. Its slow growth rate also makes it a strong candidate for bonsai cultivation.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun/Color Relationship**: Performs best with morning sun and protection from the harsh afternoon rays. In hotter climates, dappled shade is ideal to protect the delicate, curled leaves from scorching.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Ensure excellent drainage, particularly if grown in a container. A high-quality potting mix blended with pine bark is highly recommended.

> [!TIP]
> When placing 'Hippi-fi-mo' in your garden, position it somewhere you will walk by frequently. The rolled, glossy leaves are a tactile and visual treat that are best appreciated from close range!
