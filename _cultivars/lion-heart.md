## Overview
'Lion Heart' — widely sold as 'Lionheart' — is a large red laceleaf (Acer palmatum dissectum) selection noted for vigor and a unique two-stage habit. Young plants push strongly upward before the branches begin to arch and weep with age, creating a layered, cascading canopy. It reaches roughly 8-10 ft in height and width in about 15 years in Zones 5-9.

Spring color is pink-red to bright red, settling to maroon through much of the season, with bronze tones in late summer and an orange-to-crimson fall finale.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: Finely dissected leaves emerge pink-red to bright red with a soft, feathery texture.
- **Summer**: Holds maroon-red well for a dissectum; may bronze in late-summer heat. Vigorous upright shoots layer over older weeping tiers.
- **Fall**: Turns orange to crimson-red for a high-impact autumn display.

## Garden Use & Care
A strong specimen for a central bed, patio edge, or pond-side planting where the arching tiers can be viewed from below. Also suitable for a large low container while young. Staking a young leader accentuates the upright-then-weeping form.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Light Requirements**: Morning sun with afternoon shade in hot climates; tolerates more sun in cooler zones. Partial afternoon sun is ideal in Zone 7-8.
- **Wind Protection**: Shelter from strong drying winds to prevent tip burn on the fine lace leaves.
- **Pruning**: Late-winter umbrella pruning — remove straight-up water sprouts and crossing interior wood to emphasize the layered weeping habit.

> [!TIP]
> Let 'Lion Heart' establish a central leader for the first few years, then allow it to cascade. The contrast between the upright youth and weeping maturity is the cultivar's signature.
