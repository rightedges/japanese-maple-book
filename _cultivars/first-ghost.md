## Overview
'First Ghost' is the original and perhaps most famous member of Flora Wonder’s "Ghost" series. It is legendary for its spectacular reticulated variegation, where cream-white leaves are overlaid with a prominent and intricate network of dark green veins. It is a tree of immense delicacy and beauty, appearing like a misty apparition in the shade garden.

The tree has a compact, upright, and somewhat bushy habit. It is slower growing than many standard Palmatums, eventually forming a dense, architectural specimen that is highly prized by collectors of variegated maples.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: A breathtaking emergence. New leaves burst forth in pure white or creamy-yellow, with faint pink-to-red tips and a stark network of dark green veins. This reticulation is at its peak in early spring, creating a visual effect that is truly unique.
- **Summer**: The white tones slowly mature into a soft, sophisticated light green or chartreuse, while the dark veins remain visible, providing continued interest. In high-quality shade, the tree maintains a fresh, luminous look through the summer.
- **Fall**: A dramatic finale in shades of bright yellow, burnt orange, and often a soft, warm red. The fall display is multi-toned and provides a sophisticated contrast to its ethereal spring appearance.

## Garden Use & Care
'First Ghost' should be used as a focal point in a woodland garden or a protected courtyard where its intricate leaf patterns can be appreciated up close. It is particularly effective when planted against a dark background, such as a yew hedge or a shaded stone wall, where its "ghostly" white leaves can truly shine.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Protection**: Critical for success. This cultivar is extremely sensitive to direct sun, which will cause the white pigments to bleach or the leaf margins to "crisp." Plant in a spot with filtered light or bright, deep shade.
- **Moisture & Humidity**: Prefers consistent moisture and a somewhat humid environment. In dry climates, the delicate foliage may struggle; regular misting or a thick layer of mulch can help provide a more stable microclimate.
- **Minimal Pruning**: Very little pruning is required. Focus on removing any interior deadwood in late winter to maintain air circulation.

> [!TIP]
> Think of 'First Ghost' as a "living piece of lace." Plant it in the quietest, most shaded corner of your garden, and it will reward you with an ethereal beauty that is unmatched in the maple world.
