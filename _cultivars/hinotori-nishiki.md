## Overview
'Hinotori Nishiki' is a spectacular, upright-growing Japanese maple known for its breathtaking spring colors. Translating roughly to "Firebird Brocade," this cultivar lives up to its name by emerging in a fiery explosion of pink, red, and coral tones.

As a relatively compact and somewhat dense tree, it fits perfectly into smaller modern gardens while offering a dynamic, changing display of color throughout the entire growing season.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: The tree's absolute peak. Leaves emerge in vivid shades of pink, coral, and red, creating a stunning visual impact that rivals the best spring-blooming shrubs.
- **Summer**: The intense pinks gradually fade, and the foliage transitions to a darker green, often retaining delicate pinkish accents or margins on the newer growth. 
- **Fall**: The tree transforms once again, putting on a warm and vibrant display of orange, red, and pink autumn hues.
- **Form**: Develops an upright, slightly bushy habit that usually stays within the 6 to 10-foot range, making it highly manageable.

## Garden Use & Care
'Hinotori Nishiki' is best used as a specimen tree where its spring colors can take center stage. Because the spring foliage is relatively delicate, it is best planted in a sheltered location away from strong, drying winds.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun/Color Relationship**: To preserve the delicate pink spring colors for as long as possible, provide morning sun with ample afternoon shade. Too much direct sun can cause the variegated parts of the leaves to burn, while too much shade may dull the initial spring flush.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Thrives in rich, well-draining soil with even moisture. Mulching heavily is recommended to keep the root zone cool during the summer.

> [!WARNING]
> Like many highly variegated or pink-emerging maples, 'Hinotori Nishiki' is sensitive to late spring frosts. If a late freeze is forecasted right as the buds are opening, consider covering the tree overnight to protect the spectacular spring display!
