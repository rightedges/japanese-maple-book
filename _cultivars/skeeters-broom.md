## Overview
'Skeeter's Broom' is a premier dwarf red cultivar originally discovered as a witch's broom on a 'Bloodgood'. It is prized for its extraordinary vertical habit—it grows more like a narrow pillar than a rounded mound, making it a "must-have" for gardeners with limited horizontal space. It is exceptionally sun-tolerant and maintains its deep, saturated burgundy-red color remarkably well through the heat of summer.

The tree has a naturally well-branched, somewhat irregular vertical habit that develops into a dense, architectural specimen. It is more vigorous and faster-growing than many other dwarfs, making it a "rewarding" choice for quick vertical interest.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: New leaves emerge in a brilliant, shocking arrangement of burnt maroon and bright red. The foliage is typical of the Palmatum group but features slightly narrower lobes, giving the tree a refined, high-quality look.
- **Summer**: Outstanding color stability. 'Skeeter's Broom' holds its dark burgundy-red color remarkably well through the heat of summer, rarely "bronzing" or turning green even in full sun settings.
- **Fall**: A dramatic finale in shades of brilliant scarlet-red and orange. The fall display is exceptionally bright and provides a long-lasting show before the leaves drop.
- **Form**: Develops a dense, vertical structure that provides an excellent architectural "pillar" in the winter landscape.

## Garden Use & Care
Due to its unique vertical habit (6-8 feet tall, only 3-4 feet wide), 'Skeeter's Broom' is the premier choice for narrow garden beds, as a "pillar" at the corner of a house, or as a focal point in a small residential courtyard. It is also an exceptional candidate for long-term container gardening, where its verticality provides a stunning visual contrast to lower-growing plants.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Tolerance**: This is one of the best reds for full sun in the dwarf group. In most climates (Zones 5-8), it can handle direct sun with ease, provided it has consistent moisture.
- **Verticality Management**: To maintain its narrow "pillar" shape, perform light structural thinning in late winter to remove any branches that are growing too wide or crossing through the center.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Requires well-draining, slightly acidic soil. Like all Palmatums, it is sensitive to "wet feet," so ensure the planting site is naturally well-drained.

> [!TIP]
> If you have a space that needs "height without width," 'Skeeter's Broom' is the absolute champion of the genre. It delivers a vertical ruby presence that is unmatched in the dwarf world.
