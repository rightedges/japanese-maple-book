## Overview
'Bonfire' is a classic cultivar celebrated for its intense seasonal color shifts. It lives up to its name with a "fiery" spring display and a dramatic autumn finale. Its compact, upright form makes it an excellent choice for smaller landscapes and containers where its vivid performance can be a focal point.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: A spectacular emergence. New leaves burst forth in bright shades of pink, orange, and coral-red, creating a multi-toned "bonfire" effect across the canopy.
- **Summer**: The foliage transitions to a lush, deep green, sometimes with subtle bluish undertones. This green phase provides a calm, cooling presence in the mid-summer garden.
- **Fall**: The tree concludes the season with a brilliant display of crimson and scarlet-red, recapturing the intensity of its spring color.

## Garden Use & Care
With a manageable height of 6-10 feet, 'Bonfire' is ideal for urban gardens, courtyard spaces, or as a specimen plant in a mixed border. Its slender, graceful branches move well in the wind, adding a dynamic texture to the garden.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Light Requirements**: Thrives in full sun to partial shade. In hotter climates (Zones 8-9), providing afternoon shade is essential to prevent leaf scorch and keep the summer green vibrant.
- **Soil & Water**: Prefers well-draining, slightly acidic soil. Maintain consistent moisture; while established trees are somewhat drought-tolerant, prolonged dry spells will cause leaf edge crisping.
- **Pruning**: Low maintenance. Prune lightly in late summer to refine its shape if necessary, but avoid heavy spring pruning which can cause excessive sap loss.

> [!TIP]
> Site 'Bonfire' near a seating area or path where you can enjoy the translucent quality of its spring leaves as they filter the morning light.
