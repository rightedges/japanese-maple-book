## Overview
'Ryusen' is a revolutionary cultivar that has redefined what a weeping maple can be. Historically, all strictly weeping maples belonged to the Dissectum (lace-leaf) group. 'Ryusen' is the first and most successful weeping Palmatum, featuring standard, healthy green leaves on branches that drape straight down like a waterfall. It is prized for its extraordinary "strictly weeping" habit and its vigorous, robust growth.

The tree develops a strong vertical trunk (if staked) with branches that cascade directly towards the ground. It offers an unparalleled level of architectural movement and "waterfall" texture in the garden.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: New leaves emerge in a bright, clean chartreuse or light green. The foliage is typical of the Palmatum group but features unusually long and elegant petioles that contribute to the "cascading" movement of the branches.
- **Summer**: The leaves mature into a solid, healthy emerald-green. The tree forms a dense, cooling "curtain" of green that holds its color flawlessly through the heat of summer.
- **Fall**: A breathtaking grand finale. The foliage transitions into a brilliant display of electric orange and burnt gold. The fall colors are exceptionally vivid and provide a long-lasting show of warm, glowing tones.
- **Winter Silhoutte**: After the leaves fall, the strictly weeping, downward-sweeping branches are at their most visible, providing a stunning and unique silhouette that looks like a frozen waterfall.

## Garden Use & Care
'Ryusen' should be used as a central specimen tree where its "cascade" effect can be fully appreciated. It works perfectly as a focal point in a lawn, near a large stone feature, or as a graceful element leaning over a path or a wall. Because it is strictly weeping, it can be "trained" to various heights or even grown over an arbor.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Training**: Because 'Ryusen' has no central leader of its own, it must be staked to the desired height. Once the stake is removed, the head will gracefully arch over and begin its downward cascade.
- **Sun Tolerance**: This is one of the most sun-tolerant green cultivars. In most climates (Zones 5-8), it can handle full sun without significant leaf burn, provided it has consistent moisture.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Requires well-draining, slightly acidic soil. Like all Palmatums, it is sensitive to "wet feet," so ensure the planting site is naturally well-drained.

> [!TIP]
> Site 'Ryusen' at the top of a retaining wall or in a tall decorative pot. This historical planting technique allows the "Dragon's Spring" branches to drop even further, emphasizing the tree's natural waterfall movement.
