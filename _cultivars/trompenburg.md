## Overview
'Trompenburg' is one of the most distinctive and highly prized upright red cultivars in the world. Originally discovered at the Trompenburg Arboretum in the Netherlands, it is famous for its extraordinarily "cupped" leaf lobes. Each lobe of the palmate leaf curves downward at the edges, giving the entire leaf a unique, "claw-like" or convex appearance. It is prized for its exceptional vigor and its ability to maintain a deep, saturated purple-red color even in high heat.

The tree develops a strong, upright vase shape with a broad, well-structured canopy. It is more robust and faster-growing than many other red cultivars, making it a "statement" tree for any larger landscape.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: New leaves emerge in a brilliant, shocking arrangement of burnt maroon and dark purple. The "cupped" texture is immediately apparent as the leaves unfold, giving the dense canopy a unique, textured appearance early in the season.
- **Summer**: Outstanding color stability. 'Trompenburg' holds its dark purple-red or "purple-black" color remarkably well through the heat of summer, rarely "bronzing" or turning green even in full sun settings.
- **Fall**: A dramatic finale in shades of brilliant crimson and fire-engine red. The fall display is exceptionally bright and provides a long-lasting show before the leaves drop.
- **Form**: Develops a well-balanced, multi-stemmed structure that emphasizes the unique "claw-like" leaves as the tree matures.

## Garden Use & Care
'Trompenburg' is a premier specimen for a large garden, a lawn centerpiece, or as a structural element in a large mixed border. Its unique leaf texture and dark color provide a level of "visual weight" that makes it a certified conversation piece. It pairs beautifully with yellow-leaved companions or light-colored stone work.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Tolerance**: This is one of the best reds for full sun. In most climates (Zones 5-8), it can handle direct sun with ease, provided it has consistent moisture.
- **Space Management**: Give this tree plenty of vertical and horizontal room. Its large leaves and vigor mean it will eventually command a significant amount of space.
- **Minimal Pruning**: Its natural form is strong and well-balanced. Very little structural pruning is required, though a light thinning in late winter can help maintain air circulation.

> [!TIP]
> Think of 'Trompenburg' as "texture you can see from across the garden." The way the cupped purple leaves catch the light creates a visual depth and sheen that standard flat-leaved red maples cannot match.
