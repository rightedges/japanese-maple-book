## Overview
'Omure yama', which translates to "Mount Omure" (a famous volcanic hill in Japan), is one of the most elegant and unique upright cultivars. It is famous for its "downward sweep" or "cascading" branches that give the tree a soft, graceful appearance resembling a weeping willow but on an upright frame. It is prized for its refined, healthy green foliage and its extraordinary fall color.

The tree develops a strong, upright center with long, arching branches that drape towards the ground as they mature. It is exceptionally vigorous and robust, making it a "statement" tree for any larger landscape.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: New leaves emerge in a bright, clean chartreuse or light green. The foliage is typical of the Palmatum group but features unusually long and elegant petioles that contribute to the "weeping" movement of the branches.
- **Summer**: The leaves mature into a solid, healthy emerald-green. The tree maintains a dense, airy canopy that provides excellent shade. Even in high heat, 'Omure yama' remains vibrant and lush.
- **Fall**: A breathtaking grand finale. The foliage transitions into a brilliant display of electric orange, burnt gold, and saturated scarlet-red. The fall color is exceptionally vivid and provides a long-lasting show of fire-like tones.
- **Architecture**: In winter, the weeping, downward-sweeping branches are at their most visible, providing a stunning and unique silhouette against the sky.

## Garden Use & Care
'Omure yama' should be used as a central specimen tree where its scale (12-15 feet) and unique "cascading" habit can be fully appreciated. It works perfectly as a focal point in a lawn, near a large stone feature, or as a graceful element leaning over a path. Its fast growth rate also makes it a good choice for those who want a significant presence quickly.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Space Management**: Give this tree plenty of room! Its arching branches will eventually spread wide, and its beauty is best appreciated when it is not crowded by neighboring plants.
- **Sun Tolerance**: This is one of the most sun-tolerant green cultivars. In most climates (Zones 5-8), it can handle full sun without significant leaf burn, provided it has consistent moisture.
- **Pruning**: Minimal pruning is required. To maintain the cascading effect, remove any branches that are growing straight up from the top of the arches, allowing the downward-sweeping branches to take center stage.

> [!TIP]
> Plant 'Omure yama' on a small rise or near a retaining wall. This allows the cascading branches to drop even further, emphasizing the tree's natural "waterfall" movement.
