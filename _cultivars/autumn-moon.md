## Overview
'Autumn Moon' is one of the most celebrated members of the *Acer shirasawanum* family. While it shares the beautiful, fan-like leaf shape of the famous 'Aureum', it offers a unique color palette and significantly better sun tolerance. It is prized for its "peach-glow" foliage, which features layers of yellow, orange, and soft pink that shift depending on the amount of light it receives.

The tree has a graceful, upright habit that matures into a sturdy, well-structured specimen. It is more vigorous than 'Aureum', making it a more accessible choice for gardeners who want the *Shirasawanum* look without the extreme sensitivity.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: Emerges with a stunning and complex flush of peach, orange, and soft yellow. This "peach" glow is most intense on the newer growth, creating a multi-layered effect that is unlike any other maple.
- **Summer**: In moderate sun, the foliage develops its famous "sun-kissed" look—a base of chartreuse with a persistent orange-peach cast on the upper leaves. In deeper shade, it will lean more towards a clean, bright green, but it almost always retains its unique warmth.
- **Fall**: A high-impact display of electric orange, burnt gold, and brilliant scarlet-red. The fall colors are exceptionally vibrant and provide a long-lasting show that stands out even in a garden full of maples.
- **Form**: Creates a dense, bushy, upright mound with tiered branching that provides structural interest even in winter.

## Garden Use & Care
'Autumn Moon' is a versatile centerpiece. It works beautifully as a specimen plant, providing a warm, illuminated focal point in morning sun settings. It is also an excellent choice for a large container or as a component of a tall mixed border. Because of its unique "peach" tones, it pairs exceptionally well with purple-leaved cultivars like 'Bloodgood' or deep blue conifers.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun/Color Relationship**: Finding the "Goldilocks" zone of light is key. To get the best peach-and-orange summer tones, the tree needs several hours of morning sun. If kept in deep shade, it will stay mostly green. However, avoid intense late-afternoon sun in hot climates to prevent leaf scorch.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Requires well-draining, slightly acidic soil. Like all *Shirasawanums*, it is sensitive to "wet feet," so ensure the planting site is mounded or naturally well-drained.
- **Vigor**: More robust than many of its kin, but still benefits from a steady moisture regime and an annual application of balanced organic fertilizer in early spring.

> [!TIP]
> If your 'Autumn Moon' is looking too green in mid-summer, it may need just a bit more morning light. Moving a container specimen or carefully thinning surrounding branches can "turn on" the peach-orange glow.
