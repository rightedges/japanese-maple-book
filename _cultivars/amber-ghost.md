## Overview
'Amber Ghost' is a standout in Flora Wonder’s famous "Ghost" series, featuring rich amber and coral tones in the spring that contrast sharply with deep green veins. It offers a warmth and luminosity that is truly unique among reticulated variegated maples. While many ghosts lean towards cooler whites and pinks, 'Amber Ghost' brings a "sunset" palette into the shade garden, illuminating dark corners with its glowing foliage.

The tree has a graceful, upright habit that matures into a well-branched, broad-topped specimen. Its growth is sturdy and predictable, making it a reliable choice for gardeners who want a vertical element in a woodland setting.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: A breathtaking emergence. The leaves unfold in a complex tapestry of amber, coral, and soft peach, all overlaid with a prominent network of dark green veins. This "reticulation" is most intense in the first month of growth and is often described as looking like a delicate stained-glass window.
- **Summer**: As the season progresses, the amber tones soften into a sophisticated light green or greenish-gold. The dark veins remain visible, though less starkly than in spring. The tree maintains a lush, healthy appearance even in high humidity, provided it is protected from direct heat.
- **Fall**: A spectacular grand finale. The foliage transitions into a brilliant display of bright golds, burnished oranges, and often fiery, translucent reds. The late-season color is exceptionally long-lasting, making it a focal point of the autumn landscape.

## Garden Use & Care
'Amber Ghost' adds a warm, glowing quality to shaded areas and is best used as a specimen plant where its intricate leaf patterns can be appreciated up close. It is particularly effective when planted against a dark background, such as a yew hedge or a shaded stone wall, where the light-colored leaves can "pop" visually.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Light Requirements**: Critical for success. This cultivar absolutely requires protection from the hot afternoon sun. High-filtered shade or morning sun only is ideal. Too much sun will cause the delicate amber pigments to bleach or the leaf margins to "crisp."
- **Moisture Management**: Like most reticulated maples, it prefers consistent moisture. The roots should never dry out completely, but the soil must be well-draining to prevent root rot. A thick layer of pine bark mulch helps regulate soil temperature and moisture.
- **Pruning**: Minimal pruning is needed. Focus on removing dead interior twigs in late winter to maintain air circulation. If structural pruning is required to maintain its upright form, do so in mid-summer after the first flush of growth has hardened.

> [!TIP]
> Plant 'Amber Ghost' near dark-foliaged companion plants like *Heuchera* 'Obsidian' or in a dark corner of the garden. The contrast will make the tree appear to "glow" from within, even on overcast days.
