## Overview
'Hime-shojo', which translates to "Little Red Princess," is a premier dwarf red cultivar that is highly prized for its exceptional color retention and compact, bushy habit. Unlike many red maples that struggle to hold their color in lower light, 'Hime-shojo' maintains a remarkably consistent deep-red hue throughout the growing season. It is a slow to moderate grower, eventually forming a dense, architectural mound that fits perfectly into small gardens.

The tree has a naturally rounded and well-branched structure. Its leaves are smaller than the standard 'Bloodgood', giving it a refined, high-quality look that is ideal for up-close appreciation.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: New leaves emerge in a brilliant, shocking scarlet or maroon-red. The foliage is relatively small and appears in dense clusters along the branches, creating a cloud of intense red that signals the peak of spring.
- **Summer**: Outstanding color stability. While many red dwarfs fade to bronze or green, 'Hime-shojo' holds its dark burgundy-red color remarkably well. Even in partial shade, it remains a saturated red.
- **Fall**: A dramatic grand finale. The foliage turns into a vivid, translucent fire-engine red. The fall display is exceptionally bright and provides a long-lasting show before the leaves drop.
- **Form**: Develops a dense, twiggy structure that provides excellent winter interest even after the leaves have fallen.

## Garden Use & Care
Due to its small size (4-6 feet) and intense color, 'Hime-shojo' is a premier choice for rock gardens, small residential courtyards, or as a focal point in a mixed border. It is also an exceptional candidate for bonsai and long-term container gardening, where its naturally compact habit and small leaves are easily maintained.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Light Requirements**: Performs best in morning sun with afternoon shade. While it holds color well in shade, some direct morning light will intensify its saturated red hues.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Requires well-draining, slightly acidic soil. Like most dwarf cultivars, it is sensitive to "wet feet," so ensure the planting site is naturally well-drained.
- **Minimal Pruning**: Very little pruning is required. Its naturally rounded habit is self-maintaining, but a light thinning of the interior in late winter can help maintain air circulation and prevent fungal issues.

> [!TIP]
> Think of 'Hime-shojo' as a "compact Bloodgood." If you love the look of a red upright maple but don't have the space for a 20-foot tree, this "Little Princess" is the perfect solution.
