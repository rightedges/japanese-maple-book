## Overview
'Tiger Rose' is a distinctive reticulated variegated selection with a leaf form leaning strongly toward the matsumurae end of the palmatum spectrum, setting it apart from most cream-centered ghost types. It leafs out in early spring with a rose blush that quickly resolves into tiger-like striping of dark green veins on a yellow-green ground.

A signature trait is its exceptionally floriferous habit: nearly every branch carries small bouquets of red samaras that read like tiny 'roses' against the variegated foliage. It reaches 10-12 ft in 20 years, making it an ideal mid-sized understory tree for Zones 5-9.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: Emerges with a rose blush, fading to dark-green-on-yellow-green tiger reticulation. Profuse clusters of red flowers/samaras decorate the branches.
- **Summer**: Holds its tiger-striped reticulation best with protection from hot afternoon sun. Foliage remains yellow-green with dark veining.
- **Fall**: Turns shades of orange and red, differentiating it from many ghost-series cultivars that finish golden-yellow.

## Garden Use & Care
Use as a mid-sized specimen for an understory planting where the spring flowers and summer reticulation can be seen up close. Combines well with dark evergreen backdrops that make the light variegation pop.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Light Requirements**: Morning sun with afternoon shade. Hot afternoon sun bleaches the reticulation and scorches margins.
- **Moisture Management**: Prefers consistent, well-draining moisture. Mulch to keep roots cool.
- **Pruning**: Minimal. Thin dead interior twigs in late winter; shape only after spring growth hardens.

> [!TIP]
> Site 'Tiger Rose' where low morning light backlights the leaves — the tiger veining and red samara clusters show best with the sun behind them.
