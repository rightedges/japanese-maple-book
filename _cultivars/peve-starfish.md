## Overview
'Peve Starfish' is a unique and spectacular relatively new cultivar known for its extraordinarily "curled" leaf lobes. Each lobe of the palmate leaf curves downward and inward, resembling the legs of a starfish or a refined set of ruby claws. This creates a dense, architectural texture that is unlike any other upright red maple. It is a moderate grower, eventually forming a sturdy, upright-bushy specimen reaching 8-10 feet.

The tree is exceptionally sun-tolerant and maintains its deep, saturated burgundy-purple color remarkably well through the heat of summer. Its "ruby claw" texture provides a high-quality visual interest that is easy to appreciate up close.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: New leaves emerge in a brilliant, shocking arrangement of burnt maroon and bright red. The "starfish" curl is immediately apparent as the leaves unfold, giving the dense canopy a unique, textured appearance early in the season.
- **Summer**: Outstanding color retention. 'Peve Starfish' holds its dark purple-red color flawlessly through the heat of summer, rarely "bronzing" or turning green even in full sun settings. The foliage has a healthy, waxy sheen that reflects light beautifully.
- **Fall**: A dramatic finale in shades of brilliant scarlet-red and crimson. The fall colors are exceptionally vivid and long-lasting, providing a high-impact show.
- **Form**: Develops a well-balanced, multi-stemmed structure that emphasizes the unique "claw-like" leaves as the tree matures.

## Garden Use & Care
'Peve Starfish' is an ideal specimen for a medium-sized garden, a focal point in a mixed border, or as a structural element near a patio where its unique texture can be appreciated. Its moderate size (8-10 feet) and high-intensity color make it an excellent choice for framing a door or as a central element in a courtyard. It also performs exceptionally well in large containers.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Sun Tolerance**: This is one of the best reds for full sun. In most climates (Zones 5-8), it can handle direct sun with ease, provided it has consistent moisture.
- **Watering**: Prefers consistent moisture. While heat-tolerant, its unique curled leaves can be sensitive to extreme drought, so ensure it remains well-hydrated during dry spells.
- **Soil & Drainage**: Requires well-draining, slightly acidic soil. Like all Palmatums, it is sensitive to "wet feet," so ensure the planting site is naturally well-drained.

> [!TIP]
> Think of 'Peve Starfish' as "texture you can see from across the garden." The way the curled ruby leaves layer over one another creates a visual depth that standard flat-leaved maples cannot match.
