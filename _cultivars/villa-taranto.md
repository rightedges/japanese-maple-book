## Overview
'Villa Taranto' is one of the most elegant and sought-after members of the Linearilobum group. It is prized for its extraordinarily narrow, "spider-like" leaf lobes that unfold with a soft reddish-pink tint before maturing into a sophisticated mix of green and bronze. It has a graceful, vase-shaped habit that matures into an airy, transparent specimen reaching 6-10 feet.

The tree offers an unparalleled level of texture and transparency in the garden. Its "feathered" appearance provides a cooling visual effect while maintaining a high-quality, architectural presence that is unmatched by more traditional maples.

## Key Features & Seasonal Interest
- **Spring**: A spectacular emergence. New leaves burst forth in a soft, bright reddish-pink or "shrimp pink." The long, narrow lobes are exceptionally delicate, giving the entire tree an airy, "ghost-like" appearance early in the season.
- **Summer**: The intense pink recedes, maturing into a sophisticated palette of soft green with reddish tips or highlights. The tree maintains its airy, light-filled structure, providing a dappled-light effect for plants growing beneath it.
- **Fall**: A reliable finale of brilliant golden yellow and soft orange. The narrow lobes turn color sequentially, often creating a multi-toned, "brushed" appearance across the canopy.
- **Movement**: Its narrow lobes are extremely light, allowing even the slightest breeze to create a soft, rhythmic movement across the tree.

## Garden Use & Care
'Villa Taranto' should be used as a focal point near a path, a patio, or a seating area where its unique "feathered" texture and movement can be appreciated. It is an exceptional candidate for container gardening, where its airy habit and moderate growth rate make it a perfect, long-term mobile specimen. Its transparency allows you to see through the canopy to plants positioned behind it, creating a sense of depth.

### Maintenance Tips
- **Wind Protection**: The "spider-like" lobes are extremely delicate and can be sensitive to strong, drying winds which can cause "leaf shredding." Plant in a sheltered location with good air circulation.
- **Sun Tolerance**: Surprisingly sun-tolerant for such a fine-textured tree. In most climates (Zones 5-8), it can handle significant morning and midday sun, though it appreciates some break from the harshest afternoon heat.
- **Minimal Pruning**: Naturally maintains a graceful vase shape. Only prune to remove dead wood or occasional crossing branches in late winter.

> [!TIP]
> Use 'Villa Taranto' to create "layers" in your garden. Plant it in front of a dark backdrop or a solid-green shrub; its airy transparency will let you see the layers behind it, adding immense sophistication and depth to your landscape.
